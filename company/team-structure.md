# Team Structure

## Platform Engineering

Responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the cloud platform used by development teams.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Terraform module development
- AWS infrastructure management
- Amazon EKS administration
- Platform automation
- GitOps enablement
- CI/CD platform maintenance

---

## Cloud Operations

Responsible for day-to-day platform operations.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Infrastructure monitoring
- Incident response
- Production deployments
- Operational validation
- Performance monitoring
- Capacity planning

---

## Security Engineering

Responsible for protecting the cloud platform and ensuring compliance.

Primary responsibilities include:

- IAM governance
- Secrets management
- Security policy enforcement
- Vulnerability management
- Compliance reviews
- Security monitoring

---

## Development Teams

Application teams consume the shared platform to build and deploy services.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Application development
- Git-based deployments
- Service ownership
- Application monitoring
- Issue remediation

---

## Product Management

Coordinates platform priorities with engineering teams.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Roadmap planning
- Feature prioritization
- Cross-team coordination
- Stakeholder communication
